Events

Are you looking for a fresh and affordable wedding venue on the west side of Weber or Davis county? We make it happen! Crane Field is perfect for your banquets, receptions, and weddings. We have an elegantly decorated interior which is much classier than a gym or rec. center! Easily host a reception or wedding ceremony in a perfect setting with green surroundings and ample parking.

Weddings

Visit Crane Field in person during business hours, or set up a clubhouse tour before your date is taken. We can show you around the venue and help add magic touches to your wedding day.

Pricing

All packages require a Non-Refundable Booking Fee of $100 in addition to package price and sales taxes. Also, a Clubhouse Policy Agreement must be signed to complete your reservation. Full payment is required at least 90 days in advance. All sales are final. Cancellations will be reimbursed in golf shop credits only.
