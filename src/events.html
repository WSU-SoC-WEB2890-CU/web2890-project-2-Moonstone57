Weddings

Are you looking for a fresh and affordable wedding venue on the west side of Weber or Davis county? We make it happen! Crane Field is perfect for your banquets, receptions, and weddings. We have an elegantly decorated interior which is much classier than a gym or rec. center! Easily host a reception or wedding ceremony in a perfect setting with green surroundings and ample parking. Visit Crane Field in person during business hours, or set up a clubhouse tour before your date is taken. We can show you around the venue and help add magic touches to your wedding day.

Pricing

All packages require a Non-Refundable Booking Fee of $100 in addition to package price and sales taxes. Also, a Clubhouse Policy Agreement must be signed to complete your reservation. Full payment is required at least 90 days in advance. All sales are final. Cancellations will be reimbursed in golf shop credits only.

Booking

Our great hall at Crane Field is available for hosting wedding ceremonies and large receptions, accommodating up to 200 guests. The clubhouse is available for rent year-round, excluding dates already booked for events such as golf tournaments or other weddings. All events must conclude by 10 PM.

You can complete most of the booking process online if you’re ready to make full payment. Alternatively, you can make credit card payments over the phone or in person at the clubhouse. For partial payments, please arrange a payment schedule by phone or in person.
