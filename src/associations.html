Handicap Questions

No, we keep track of in-house handicaps so you don’t need to be a UGA member. If you are new we can use your current handicap index a a benchmark to start from. Otherwise you will have to turn in a couple rounds of golf first to establish a handicap.

Members with UGA handicaps may use their index to immediately establish a house handicap, but you do not have to be a UGA membership to play. If you do not have a UGA membership you simply need to play two 18 hole rounds (or two nine hole scores on each side) to establish an in-house index. Not difficult at all, and we do all the work entering your scores each week to keep your handicap accurate. Once established, your handicap is used for Low Net events and for determining flight divisions.

Flights are automatically generated by the computer system every week. Flight 1 contains the lowest handicaps, Flight 2 contains the next lowest, and so forth. As the individuals who play vary from week to week, this can result in a player being occasionally assigned to a flight they are not accustomed to playing.

As most of our association events are 9-hole events, our handicaps are kept as 9-hole handicaps. When we have 18-hole events, we double the handicaps.

When using handicaps, the tees you play will not give you much of an advantage. The slopes for each Tee are used in the handicap calculation. If someone plays from forward tees and consistently shoots lower scores on average, a lower handicap will result, and level out the advantage.

Handicap calculations are based upon the 10 most recent scores (minus the score that is still in process of being scored.) Every time a new score is entered the oldest score is dropped. Sometimes this results in handicap movement in the opposite direction of what is expected based only on the most recent score. Handicap calculation also drops high and low scores which helps prevent sandbagging.

No, we start with your previous year handicap. You do not start at zero unless you are a brand new player without any benchmark handicap index such as a UGA index or a handicap from another association.

Yes, the most recent copy is posted online and in the Facebook Group page. You can also request a copy through the website contact-us page. No handicap system is perfect, but we have researched many different software solutions and landed on the current in-house calculator. Rest assured it is evenly applied to all members in the association.

No, we keep our in in-house handicap indexes. They are used to calculate net scores on the majority of our tournament events. They are based off tournament rounds only, and are more accurate.
