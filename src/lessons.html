Lessons

Personalized One-On-One Lessons

If you are ready to shave points off your scorecard and get serious about your golf game, golf lesson packages will really make a difference. For anything worth doing, it is worth doing right, which is why we recommend the 10 or 20 lesson package first and foremost. This package can shave 5-10 points off your score. Your instructor will work one on one with you and keep track of your progress in a systematic way.

If time is a constraint we also offer 1, 3, and 5 lesson packages that will give you an edge over 90 percent of your friends. The magic of lessons, is that no one can take the knowledge away that you gain from each class. You get to hold on to it for a lifetime.

When you buy a lesson punch card, we will include a bonus bucket of range balls with every lesson! Make the decision today to get what you want, better skills and more respect. You can have it all!

Classes for Beginner Adults

Start Something New This Summer!

Our adult beginner golf course is the perfect starting point for men and women who want to learn the game in a relaxed, supportive setting. There are four classes, lasting an hour each, on Monday evenings. Enjoy a fun, encouraging, and educational environment designed to help beginners build skills and confidence from day one.

Whether you’re learning for fun, fitness, or professional networking, this course is the perfect introduction. Curriculum focuses on developing golf skills (full swing, short game, rules and etiquette) while keeping the experience fun and engaging. A fun, safe and educational environment designed with a beginner’s advancement in mind.

Junior Prep Academy Ages 11+

Master Your Game. Compete with Confidence. Achieve Your Goals.

Designed for golfers 11 and older, the Junior Prep Academy is composed of weekly classes during the summer. With a max of four students per class, personalized instruction and focused skill development is ensured.

Are you ready to take your golf game to the next level? Whether you’re preparing for high school tryouts, working toward a spot on a team, or striving to compete in tournaments, this program is designed to give you the edge you need to succeed.

This is tailored for junior golfers according to their ability. Every session is packed with personalized coaching, game-enhancing drills, and training that will elevate your skills and confidence on the course.
